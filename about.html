About Qisheng Plastic Gifts

A Hong Kong based toys manufacturer and exporter, connecting world-class China toy production with buyers in 30+ countries.

Who We Are

Play Value, Export Reliability

Qisheng Plastic Gifts Co., Limited (香港祺盛塑膠禮品有限公司) is a Hong Kong registered company specializing in the design, manufacture and export of children's plastic toys and gifts. Our production network is based in the Shantou and Dongguan toy manufacturing clusters of Guangdong, China — the world's largest plastic toys supply chain.

We serve toy importers, wholesale distributors, chain retailers, e-commerce sellers and promotional gift companies with factory-direct pricing, strict quality control and flexible OEM/ODM services. From a single carton trial order to full container programs, every shipment follows the same disciplined QC process.

Our Hong Kong trading desk handles contracts, banking and logistics coordination in English, Mandarin and Cantonese — making sourcing from China simple and transparent for overseas buyers.
