FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your MOQ?

Standard MOQ is 3–10 cartons per item depending on the product line. Trial orders and mixed-container loading are welcome — contact us for a flexible MOQ arrangement.

Do you support OEM/ODM and custom packaging?

Yes. We offer custom colors, logos, color boxes and full ODM development including new molds. Send us your design or idea and we will quote within 24 hours.

What safety certifications do your toys have?

Our products are made with compliant materials and can be tested to EN71 (EU), ASTM F963 (US) and CPSIA requirements. Test reports from SGS/Intertek are available per order.

What are your payment terms?

T/T: 30% deposit before production, balance against B/L copy. L/C at sight is accepted for orders above USD 50,000. Trade assurance orders are welcome.

What is the production lead time?

Typically 25–35 days after sample approval and deposit, depending on order quantity and season. Peak season (Aug–Nov) may add 5–10 days.

Which shipping terms do you offer?

FOB Shenzhen / Shantou, CIF, EXW and DDP by sea or air. We consolidate mixed items into full containers and handle all export documentation.

Can I get samples before ordering?

Yes. Existing samples are free of charge; you only cover the courier fee. Custom samples are quoted based on tooling requirements.

How do you control quality?

Three-stage QC: incoming material inspection, in-line production checks and 100% pre-shipment inspection with an AQL photo report sent before shipment.
